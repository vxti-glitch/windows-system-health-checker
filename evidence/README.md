# Future controlled resource-pressure lab

No lab result, screenshot, or machine report is stored here. A future genuine high-CPU or low-disk before/after record must include:

- UTC date/time, authorized lab owner, Windows version, and sanitized environment boundary.
- Baseline command and safe-share report path.
- The exact controlled CPU workload or disposable-file method used to create pressure.
- Expected effect, stop condition, data-loss boundary, and rollback/cleanup plan.
- Before, during, and after observations with sample duration and configured thresholds.
- The hypothesis being tested and alternatives the snapshot cannot distinguish.
- The authorized correction, repeated original task, and observable validation result.
- Cleanup confirmation: workload stopped, disposable files removed, free space restored, and output reviewed.
- Redaction confirmation for username, hostname, IP, process, service, storage path, installed software, internal domain, and unrelated system details.
- Limitations, including why a short lab does not prove production reliability or diagnose unrelated performance problems.

Keep raw reports outside the repository. Commit only a manually reviewed, sanitized narrative and the minimum supporting artifacts needed to trace the result.
